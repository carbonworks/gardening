# Soil Recipes

All recipes start from the same premixed base. Mix additions in *by
volume of finished mix* (so "10% perlite" means perlite makes up
roughly 10% of the final volume after mixing).

> **Important pH note.** Our premixed base measures **~7.0** —
> dominated by the alkaline mushroom compost, partially offset by
> the peat. That's:
> - **Fine** for lavender (loves it), and tolerable for herbs, lettuce, cucumber, and marigolds.
> - **Top edge** for tomatoes and peppers (target 6.0–6.8) — workable, optional sulfur if measurements stay high.
> - **Too high** for **potatoes** (scab risk) and **strawberries** — these recipes add elemental sulfur to acidify.

| **Dr Teal's Epsom Salt** | 1 tsp / gal of finished mix | magnesium, pH-neutral |

## Potato Mix

For: russet seed potatoes.

| Component | Amount | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Base mix | ~75% | |
| Perlite | +15% | loose, well-drained |
| **Extra peat moss** | +10% | immediate pH drop (peat is acidic) |
| **Earth Science Fast Acting Sulfur** | 2 tbsp / gal of finished mix | drop pH toward 5.5–6.0 over 2–4 weeks |

- **Target pH:** 5.0 – 6.0
- **Expected after amendments:** ~6.0–6.5 immediately (from extra peat); ~5.5–6.0 after 2–4 weeks (sulfur oxidation).
- **Avoid:** garden lime, Mag-I-Cal Plus. Both raise pH and encourage potato scab.
- The bundled gypsum (12% Ca) inside the sulfur product is pH-neutral — not a concern.
- **Test pH 2–3 weeks after planting** and add more sulfur if reading is still above 6.0.

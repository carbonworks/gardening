10% of the final volume after mixing).

## Tomato & Pepper Mix

For: Dora Red, Joy Red, Hartman's Yellow Gooseberry, Orange Blaze pepper, Lunch Box Orange pepper.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Base mix | ~90% |
| Perlite | +10% |
| Mag-I-Cal | 1 tbsp per gallon of finished mix |

- **Target pH:** 6.0 – 6.8
- Mag-I-Cal supplies calcium to help prevent blossom end rot.

## General Herb Mix

For: basil, lemon balm.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Base mix | ~90% |
| Perlite | +10% |

- **Target pH:** 6.0 – 7.0
- Moderate drainage. Suitable for most culinary herbs.

## Mediterranean Herb Mix (lavender)

For: lavender 'Provence'.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Base mix | ~60% |
| Perlite | +30% |
| Vermiculite | +10% |
| Garden lime | 1 tbsp per gallon of finished mix |

- **Target pH:** 6.5 – 7.5
- Heavy drainage, lean, slightly alkaline. Lavender dislikes rich, moisture-retentive soils.

## Lettuce / Salad Greens Mix

For: mesclun mix lettuce.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Base mix | ~80% |
| Vermiculite | +10% |

- **Target pH:** 6.0 – 7.0
- Extra moisture retention for shallow-rooted greens.

## Potato Mix

For: russet seed potatoes.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Base mix | ~85% |
| Perlite | +15% |

- **Target pH:** 5.0 – 6.0
- **Avoid:** garden lime, Mag-I-Cal. Both raise pH and encourage potato scab.
- Loose and well-drained.
